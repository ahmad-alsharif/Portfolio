Overview

This investigation leverages R to dissect the relationship between climatic variables and agricultural output. By correlating rainfall and temperature patterns with historical yield data, the study identifies critical environmental thresholds required to maximize productivity.

Key Findings

  • Rainfall shows a strong positive correlation with total yield (0.768).
  • Temperature fluctuations have a minimal impact (0.084) on current yield data.
  • Harvesting duration remains statistically independent of weather variables.
