About Me

I’ve always believed that data is only as good as the decisions it helps people make. With a background in Business IT from Cape Breton and a deep dive into Data Analytics at SAIT, I’ve landed in that sweet spot where business logic meets technical execution. I don't just "clean data" for the sake of it; I hunt for the stories hidden in messy datasets and turn them into clear, visual roadmaps that actually move the needle for a business.

My daily toolkit usually involves a mix of SQL, Python, and Power BI, but I see these as more than just tools—they’re how I solve puzzles. I specialize in taking the technical "heavy lifting" (like complex ETL processes and predictive modeling) and distilling it into dashboards that make sense to everyone, from the engineering team to the CEO. If a report doesn't spark an "aha!" moment, it’s not finished yet.

What makes my approach different is my history in account management and sales. I’ve spent years in the trenches of operations, so I know what it’s like to need an answer now to a real-world problem. This helps me bridge the gap that often exists between "the numbers" and "the people." I’m not just looking for a technical solution; I’m looking for a practical one that makes someone’s job easier and their strategy smarter.
